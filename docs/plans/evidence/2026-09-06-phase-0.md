## Cyrene persistence and recovery

本节记录 Cyrene 持久化与恢复的实际证据。

Navigator commit `c00d59c81feac9d682fca6a10aa92f304efaa56d`; the persistence

### Result

- The second independent Web process explicitly took over epoch 11 and returned
  `RECOVERY_OK`. It observed 55 stored events, preserved the original 20-event
  prefix event-for-event, and retained one historical Tool Call/Result pair.
- The persistence service was SIGKILLed and restarted. The final recovery record

  established.
- The 20-event append proof and the restart records establish ordered event
  persistence, writer epoch fencing and a commit-visible flush boundary. The
  Python service test matrix also covers idempotent same-body batch retry and
  conflicting batch rejection as atomic operations.

### Environment and limitations

The two Web clients were independent processes on one WSL2 host, with a separate
persistence process and SQLite WAL database. This proves service/process restart
and same-host writer fencing. P0-17 remains unchecked because no second physical

## Cancellation sub-scenario

本节记录取消子场景的实际证据。

### Result

`live-cancel-v2-result.json` is `PASS` for the bounded cancellation proof:

- The collector intentionally stopped at 1800 ms; `completedTurnEnd: false` is
  the expected cancellation result.
- 77 assistant frames arrived before the stop, cancel was accepted, and the
  Cyrene eventlog persisted 15 events ending with `user-aborted`.
- The post-cancel vLLM observation reported zero running and zero waiting
  requests; the gateway recorded no traceback, uncaught exception or
  `ProviderInvocationCancelled` error.

`live-cancel-v2-recovery.json` is `PASS` and returned `CANCEL_RECOVERY_OK` on the
same Session. A separate real CES-to-Official-HTTP check demonstrated no retry
and no fabricated usage. These records do not claim a cancellation latency
metric: the 1800 ms value is the intentional collector limit, not measured
cancel latency.

### Why P0-11 remains unchecked / P0-11 保持未勾选的原因

P0-11 also requires readable authentication, permission, token/usage and failed
request audit records in the persistent Exchange Product ledger. The current
reference configuration and usage passthrough do not yet establish that ledger,
so the completed cancel sub-scenario is recorded here without checking P0-11.
Full Usage/Cost/Audit ownership remains a later product/enterprise acceptance
item.
